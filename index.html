E2 Assistant

An AI assistant that answers questions from public environmental documents. It was designed as a test of what makes an AI tool trustworthy: it quotes its sources, cites every claim, and refuses when it cannot answer.

How it works
  • 1
    Hybrid search: finds the most relevant passages.
  • 2
    Numbered citations: it cannot cite a source it wasn't given.
  • 3
    Clear refusals: every answer marked Answered, Partial or Refused.
  • 4
    Measured errors: tested on unseen questions, weak spots published.

Environmental Burden Assessment

Shows the total pollution load a community already carries before a new major project is added. Applied to five major federal projects, including LNG Canada Phase 2 in Kitimat, BC.

How it works
  • 1
    Three public datasets: NPRI, GHGRP and NAPS combined.
  • 2
    Cumulative view: all facilities together, not one at a time.
  • 3
    Power BI dashboard: the full picture in minutes.
  • 4
    Three versions: each refined with feedback.
